Paperscart

Apr 2026 — Present · Premium paid social platform (PWA)

A premium paid social platform where every interaction carries real monetary value — a paid manifestation wall, anonymous encrypted confessions and self-commitment contracts, all running on a single wallet.

  • Complete integer wallet economy with Razorpay integration — HMAC-verified webhooks and idempotent credits across a 31-table Postgres schema.
  • Peer-to-peer creator-earnings model: community wishes and manifests flow directly to post authors, so posts can earn many multiples of their posting cost.
  • Production SSR/SEO with dynamic OG images, web push notifications and AES-256-GCM encrypted confessions.
  • 50+ early users onboarded; in user-acquisition phase ahead of the Jan 2027 real-money launch.
